Frequently Asked Questions

How is my health data secured? expand_more
MediSphere utilizes end-to-end AES-256 encryption and adheres to strict HIPAA regulatory compliance standards. Your medical records are owned exclusively by you.
Can laboratories and pharmacies register on MediSphere? expand_more
Yes. Certified laboratories and licensed medical stores can create professional accounts to manage inventories, process test bookings, and fulfill prescription requests.
How does the Emergency Health Pass work? expand_more
Each profile generates a secure Digital Health Pass QR code containing blood group, emergency contact info, and critical medical history for emergency first responders.
